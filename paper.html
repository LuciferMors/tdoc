Abstract

This study reports three mechanistically connected findings regarding FHRUC phase coupling in intrapartum labour. Fetuses destined for severe acidosis show elevated FHRUC phase coherence in the first 20 minutes of labour, confirmed by permutation testing (n=506).

Methods

We analysed 506 intrapartum CTG recordings from the CTU-UHB Intrapartum CTG Database. Instantaneous phase coherence between FHR and UC was computed using the Hilbert transform in sliding 5-minute windows.

C(t) = \left| \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^{N} e^{i \Delta\phi(t,k)} \right| Phase Coherence

Results

Fetuses with severe acidosis exhibited significantly elevated FHRUC phase coherence in the first 20 minutes.

Conclusions

Early-labour FHRUC phase hypercoupling in fetuses destined for severe acidosis represents a mechanistic signature of compensatory autonomic activation. Coherence above 0.45 carries a 5.8x relative risk of severe acidosis.
