

# Abstract

This study reports three mechanistically connected findings regarding FHRUC
phase coupling in intrapartum labour. Fetuses destined for severe acidosis show
elevated FHRUC phase coherence in the first 20 minutes of labour, confirmed by
permutation testing (n=506).



# Methods

We analysed 506 intrapartum CTG recordings from the CTU-UHB Intrapartum CTG
Database. Instantaneous phase coherence between FHR and UC was computed using
the Hilbert transform in sliding 5-minute windows.

  [Phase Coherence]  C(t) = \left| \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^{N} e^{i \Delta\phi(t,k)} \right|



# Results

Fetuses with severe acidosis exhibited significantly elevated FHRUC phase
coherence in the first 20 minutes.

+---------------------+-----+------------------+---------+
| Group               | n   | Median Coherence | p-value |
+---------------------+-----+------------------+---------+
| Normal (pH >= 7.20) | 347 | 0.250            | —       |
| Severe (pH < 7.05)  | 25  | 0.336            | 0.018   |
+---------------------+-----+------------------+---------+

[MINOR] Permutation-validated: minute 12 p=0.005, minute 18 p=0.012.



# Conclusions

Early-labour FHRUC phase hypercoupling in fetuses destined for severe acidosis
represents a mechanistic signature of compensatory autonomic activation.
Coherence above 0.45 carries a 5.8x relative risk of severe acidosis.
